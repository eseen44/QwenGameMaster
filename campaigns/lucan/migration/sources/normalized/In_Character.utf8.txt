7. **Bard** - Has instrument, musical charms and illusions, prefers distracting and tricking.
8. **Paladin** - Has holy sword, heavy armour, smiting and healing abilities, prefers frontline combat.
9. **Warlock** - Has life-draining magic, curses, dark summons, prefers weakening enemies before finishing them.
